I’m a multidisciplinary creative professional with experience in both visual design and web development. Over the years, I’ve worked on a wide range of creative projects using Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Adobe XD and Figma. My design work includes creating logos, posters and marketing materials, always with a focus on clean visuals and consistent branding. I’ve also developed engaging motion graphics and short animations using After Effects, often for promotional videos and social media content.

In the area of UI/UX design, I’ve used Adobe XD to build interactive prototypes for both websites and mobile apps—starting from low-fidelity wireframes to polished, high-fidelity mockups. I strive to create designs that are not only visually appealing but also intuitive and user-friendly.

On the development side, I have solid experience building scalable and secure web applications using PHP and the Laravel framework. My work includes writing clean and maintainable backend code, integrating RESTful APIs, optimizing database queries for performance, and collaborating in agile teams to deliver high-quality software. I’m also involved in code reviews and follow best practices to ensure clean, efficient code.

Whether I’m working on visual design, animation, or backend development, my goal is always to create work that is both beautiful and functional—bringing ideas to life in a way that’s impactful and user-centered.
